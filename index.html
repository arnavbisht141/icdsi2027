About the Conference

A Premier Interdisciplinary Forum for Global Innovators

The International Conference on Research in Artificial Intelligence, Data, Hardware and Advanced Quantum Technologies (RADHA 2027) aims to provide a world-class multidisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, and innovators.

Hosted by the Department of Information Technology at Delhi Technological University (DTU), the conference encompasses theoretical foundations, technological advancements, experimental investigations, and practical applications, with particular emphasis on the convergence of artificial intelligence, data-driven systems, advanced hardware, and quantum technologies.

RADHA 2027 seeks to encourage collaborative discovery across disciplines, bringing together scientific research communities working on both classical and quantum technologies and their applications to contemporary industrial, health, environmental, and societal challenges.

Call For Contributions

12 Technical Tracks

RADHA 2027 welcomes original contributions across 12 multidisciplinary computing and hardware domains.

Track 01

Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning & Intelligent Systems

Deep learning, LLMs, reinforcement learning, autonomous agents, explainable & neuro-symbolic AI.

Track 02

Data Science, Big Data & Advanced Analytics

Data engineering, knowledge discovery, predictive analytics, knowledge graphs, and privacy analytics.

Track 03

Quantum Computing & Quantum Information Science

Quantum algorithms, quantum ML, simulation, fault-tolerant computing, and hybrid classical-quantum models.

Track 04

Quantum Communication, Sensing & Emerging Quantum Tech

QKD, quantum cryptography, quantum metrology, optical networks, quantum materials, and device integration.

Track 05

Semiconductor Technologies, VLSI & Advanced Hardware

Electronic design automation, FPGA, SoC, chiplet packaging, energy-efficient microelectronics, and nanoelectronics.

Track 06

Advanced Computing Architectures & Distributed Systems

HPC, GPU/AI acceleration, cloud/fog computing, neuromorphic computing, and decentralized infrastructure.

Host Institution

About Delhi Technological University (DTU)

Delhi Technological University (DTU), formerly known as the historic Delhi College of Engineering (DCE, founded in 1941), is one of India's foremost engineering universities with over 80 years of excellence in technological innovation, pedagogy, and research.

Its sprawling 164-acre lush green campus in Rohini, New Delhi, houses state-of-the-art supercomputing centers, advanced semiconductor and VLSI design labs, robotics testbeds, and research hubs supported by DST, CSIR, and DRDO.

The Department of Information Technology at DTU is renowned for high-impact research in artificial intelligence, computer vision, data analytics, cyber-physical systems, and quantum communications, fostering international conferences and research partnerships.
