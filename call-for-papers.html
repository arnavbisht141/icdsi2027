Call for Papers

12 Technical Tracks and 7 Special Focus Areas across Advanced Computing and Quantum Technologies

Submission Scope

Advancing Fundamental Theory, Novel Architectures & Applications

The International Conference on Research in Artificial Intelligence, Data, Hardware and Advanced Quantum Technologies (RADHA 2027) invites original research papers presenting theoretical contributions, novel methodologies, architectures, algorithms, experimental studies, hardware prototypes, and validated real-world applications.

Prospective authors are invited to submit high-quality original research papers that have not been previously published or are under review elsewhere. Interdisciplinary submissions connecting two or more technical tracks are particularly encouraged.

Proceedings Notice: All accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion in Springer's Scopus-indexed proceedings, subject to final approval.
