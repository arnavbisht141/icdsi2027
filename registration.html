Registration Fee Schedule

Registration Entitlements & Policy:

  • Author Registration: Entitles presentation of one accepted paper, inclusion in Springer Scopus-indexed proceedings (subject to approval), full conference kit, tea/coffee breaks, conference lunch, and a formal presentation certificate.
  • Co-Author Registration: Any co-author attending or presenting requires an individual registration (or attendee registration).
  • Student Eligibility: Student delegates must present a valid institutional student ID card.
  • Hybrid Mode: Virtual participants receive an official digital presentation slot and certificate of presentation.
  • Cancellation Policy: Registration fees are non-refundable once processed.
