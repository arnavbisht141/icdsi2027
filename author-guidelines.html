Formatting Guidelines

Manuscript Preparation Instructions

Prospective authors are invited to submit full-length, original research papers. While submitting a manuscript to RADHA 2027, the authors acknowledge that no paper substantially similar in content has been or will be submitted to another journal, conference, or workshop during the review period.

Springer Conference Proceedings Guidelines

Paper Structure & Page Requirements

  • Page Length: Standard papers should be between 12 to 15 pages including references and figures. Extra pages up to 3 pages may be accepted with supplementary page charges.
  • Language & Anonymity: Submissions must be written in English. Authors should ensure manuscripts submitted for initial review follow double-blind requirements (remove author names, affiliations, and explicit self-referential funding notes).
  • Originality & Similarity: All submissions will undergo strict anti-plagiarism screening. Papers with excessive similarity scores or evidence of unacknowledged generative AI without disclosure will be rejected without review.
  • File Format: Electronic submissions must be uploaded as a single PDF document through the CMT portal.
