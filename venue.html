Transit & Connectivity

How to Reach DTU Campus

Delhi Technological University is well-connected by the modern Delhi Metro network, road arteries, and international airport terminals.

🚇

By Delhi Metro

Samaypur Badli Station (Yellow Line): Located ~3.5 km from campus. Feeder electric rickshaws and autos run continuously to DTU Main Gate.

Rithala Station (Red Line): Located ~4.5 km from campus, offering quick connectivity from West and East Delhi.

✈️

From IGI Airport (DEL)

Located approximately 32 km from DTU campus (~50–65 minutes by cab).

By Metro: Take the Airport Express Orange Line to New Delhi Station, switch to the Yellow Line northbound directly to Samaypur Badli Station.

🚆

From Railway Stations

New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS): Direct Yellow Line metro to Samaypur Badli (~35 mins).

Old Delhi Railway Station (DLI): Direct Yellow Line from Chandni Chowk to Samaypur Badli (~30 mins).

🚕

Cabs & Ridesharing

Ola, Uber, and prepaid commercial taxis operate extensively throughout Delhi-NCR directly to "Delhi Technological University, Rohini Gate 1".

🌤️

Weather in New Delhi (February):

February offers the most pleasant, crisp weather in New Delhi with sunny afternoons (18°C–24°C) and cool evenings (10°C–14°C). Light winter clothing or a jacket is recommended.
