International Participants

Visa Guidance & Official Invitation Letters

International researchers and authors travelling to India for RADHA 2027 generally require a valid Conference Visa or e-Conference Visa issued by the Government of India.

Official Invitation Letter Procedure:

  1. Ensure your accepted paper registration has been completed and fee payment verified.
  2. Send an email to radha@dtu.ac.in with subject "Visa Invitation Letter Request — Paper ID [Your ID]".
  3. Provide: Full Name as on Passport, Nationality, Passport Number, Affiliation, and Title of Accepted Paper.
  4. The conference chair will issue a formal stamped institutional invitation letter from Delhi Technological University for your visa submission.
Explore The Capital

Discovering New Delhi

Delhi combines centuries of Mughal, British, and modern Indian heritage. Take time to explore these celebrated landmarks during your conference visit:

Red Fort & Chandni Chowk

Historic 17th-century Mughal fortress and the bustling lanes of Old Delhi, renowned for authentic culinary specialties and spice markets.

Qutub Minar

UNESCO World Heritage site featuring the world's tallest brick minaret (72.5 meters), surrounded by intricately carved medieval monuments.

India Gate & Kartavya Path

The iconic 42-meter triumphal war memorial situated along the grand ceremonial avenue leading to Rashtrapati Bhavan.

Akshardham Temple

A breathtaking Hindu temple complex showcasing millennia of traditional Indian and Hindu culture, spirituality, and ornate sandstone architecture.
